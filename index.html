Antonios Argyriou

Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering

Website | LinkedIn | Twitter | arXiv | Google Scholar

I am a Professor in Electrical & Computer Engineering. My research spans wireless communications, sensing, signal processing, machine learning, and security. Over the years I have published in well-recongnized conferences (AAAI, CVPR, Infocom, ICC, Globecom) and journals (various IEEE and ACM Transactions). I am currently an Associate Editor for the IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society. My research has been funded by the European Union and the European Space Agency (ESA). I have been the recepient of a Marie Curie Award. I have held various academic positions in the past: Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of South Carolina, Philips Research, and Soft.Networks in Atlanta, GA. I have a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The Georgia Institute of Technology (Georgia Tech), Atlanta, USA.
